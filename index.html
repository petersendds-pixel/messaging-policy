Messaging line

(575) 414-1493 · Las Cruces, New Mexico

This page documents the SMS messaging practices for the line operated by Matt Petersen at (575) 414-1493.

This number is used for individual, two-way text conversations with three groups: tenants of properties Matt Petersen owns and manages, employees, and vendors and contractors. Typical subjects are maintenance and repair requests, scheduling contractor access, shift and scheduling coordination, and vendor orders and service calls.

Every message is written individually for a single named recipient. There is no contact list, no templates or merge fields, no scheduled or batch sending, and no marketing or promotional content.

No mobile information is sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

How consent is obtained

  • Tenants sign a text-message authorization addendum to their lease.
  • Employees sign a text-message acknowledgment in the employee handbook.
  • Vendors and contractors give consent verbally when they provide their mobile number while arranging service.

In every case consent is given directly by the person whose number it is. No numbers are purchased, rented, or obtained from third parties, and there is no sign-up form on this site.
