SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances, except with the messaging service providers strictly necessary to transmit the messages themselves.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes how Matt Petersen ("we", "I") collects, uses, and protects mobile phone numbers and message content in connection with the personal messaging line at (575) 414-1493. It applies to every message sent from or received at that number.

What we collect

  • Your mobile phone number, as you provided it directly to us.
  • The content of messages you send to and receive from this number.
  • Basic delivery metadata generated by the carrier, such as timestamps and delivery status.

How we obtain your number

Mobile numbers are obtained only from the individual to whom the number belongs, given directly and verbally in the course of an existing personal or professional relationship. We do not purchase, rent, lease, or otherwise acquire phone numbers from third parties, list brokers, or public sources. We do not collect phone numbers through any web form, and we do not run any public sign-up.

How we use your number

Your number is used solely to exchange individual, conversational messages with you. Message frequency varies and depends entirely on the conversation; there is no fixed or recurring send schedule. We do not send bulk, promotional, advertising, or telemarketing messages to this list.

How we share your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade mobile phone numbers. We do not share mobile numbers, opt-in data, or consent records with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The only disclosure that occurs is to our messaging infrastructure provider, Twilio Inc., which transmits and receives the messages on our behalf and is contractually bound to process that data only for that purpose. We may also disclose information where required by law or valid legal process.

Opting out

You may stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from this number. You will receive a single confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us using the details below. Opt-out requests are honored automatically and immediately at the carrier level.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message frequency varies.

Data retention and security

Message content and phone numbers are retained only as long as needed for the ongoing conversation and for any recordkeeping required by law, and are then deleted. Access is limited to Matt Petersen and to automated tooling operating under his direction. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, though no method of transmission over a mobile network is entirely secure.

Protected health information

This is a personal line and is not an approved channel for protected health information. Please do not send medical, dental, or other health information to this number. Any such message will be redirected to the appropriate office contact and will not be used to provide clinical care or advice.

Children

This messaging line is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revision date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change.
