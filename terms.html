Program description

Matt Petersen operates a messaging line at (575) 414-1493 for individual, two-way conversation with three groups:

  • Tenants of properties he owns and manages — maintenance and repair requests, scheduling contractor access, and property coordination.
  • Employees — scheduling and shift coverage, facility and equipment issues, and day-to-day operational coordination.
  • Vendors and contractors — orders, quotes, service appointments, and delivery coordination.

Every message is composed individually for a single named recipient. There is no contact list, no templates or merge fields, no scheduled or batch sending, and no message is sent to more than one recipient. Some replies are drafted with the assistance of automated tooling operating under Matt Petersen's direction; a human remains responsible for the account. This is not a marketing, advertising, or promotional program, and no promotional messages are sent from this line.

Consent

You are receiving messages because you gave your mobile number and consent directly, in one of the following ways:

  • Tenants: by signing the text-message authorization addendum to your lease.
  • Employees: by signing the text-message acknowledgment in the employee handbook.
  • Vendors and contractors: verbally, when you provided your mobile number while arranging service or placing an order and were told the number is used for text coordination.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of tenancy, of employment, or of any purchase or service. No numbers are obtained from third parties, purchased lists, or public sources.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and is determined by the conversation. There is no recurring or scheduled send. In a typical month a recipient may receive anywhere from zero messages to a normal volume of back-and-forth conversation.

Cost

There is no charge from us for these messages. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile plan and carrier. Please consult your carrier for details on your plan.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. After you send STOP you will receive one confirmation message and will not receive further messages from this number. Opt-out is processed automatically at the carrier level and takes effect immediately. Opting out will not affect your lease, your employment, or your account; we will simply contact you by phone, email, or written notice instead.
